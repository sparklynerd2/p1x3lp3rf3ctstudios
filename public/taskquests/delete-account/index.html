TaskQuests Planner & Randomizer

Delete your account

Applies to the Android app TaskQuests Planner & Randomizer (com.neurodiverged.android) · Last updated: November 9, 2026

You can ask us to delete your TaskQuests Planner & Randomizer account and all the data synced with it at any time, even if you no longer have the app installed.

Never signed in?

Then there's nothing on our servers to delete. As a guest, your data lives only on your phone. Uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings › Apps › TaskQuests Planner & Randomizer › Storage › Clear data, to remove it permanently.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (user ID, email address, display name and profile photo).
  • All synced app content: quests, to-dos, habits, notes, focus sessions, lists, appointments, money entries, XP, levels, gold, streaks and settings.
  • Any pending partner invite codes. If you're paired with a partner, you're removed from the shared household; your partner keeps their own copy of what you shared.

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. Data stored on your own device isn't affected: uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove it. Residual copies in encrypted backups are removed within 30 days.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you, except anything we're legally required to keep. Purchase records held by Google Play are managed by Google.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form below and send the email it creates, from the email address you signed in with. That's how we confirm the request is yours.
  2. We delete your account and data and email you to confirm, within 30 days and usually much sooner.

Request deletion

Send the request from this address so we can verify it's you.

This opens your email app with the request filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send.

Prefer to write it yourself? Email support@task-quests.com with the subject Delete my TaskQuests account.

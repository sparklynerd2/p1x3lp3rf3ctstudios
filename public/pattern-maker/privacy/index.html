Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information Diamond Painting Pattern Maker handles, why, and the choices you have. Diamond Painting Pattern Maker is made by P1x3l P3rf3ct Studios ("we", "us").

The short version

  • The app runs entirely on your device: no account, no analytics, no ads.
  • Your photos and patterns are never uploaded. Exports go only where you send them.
  • We collect no personal information.

1. Information we collect

None. Diamond Painting Pattern Maker works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting tools, and it does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

2. Your photos and patterns

When you choose a photo, it is opened from your device and turned into a pattern on your device. The photo, the pattern and the color key are never uploaded. When you export or share a pattern (PNG) or color key (CSV), the file is saved to your device or handed to the app you choose in Android's share sheet; what happens to it next is up to you and that app. Printing uses Android's print service.

3. Device permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location or contacts. Photos are chosen through Android's own file picker, which gives the app only the photo you select. Android lists an Internet permission for the app because it is built on a web view, but the app does not use it to send your data anywhere.

4. Purchases

If you buy the app or an upgrade through Google Play, Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

5. Desktop edition

The Windows desktop edition of the pattern maker is sold through Gumroad. To activate it, the license key you enter is checked with Gumroad's license API. The app stores the activation (your license key, the email address Gumroad returns for the purchase, and a scrambled machine fingerprint so the activation can't be copied to another computer) in a file on your computer only. Your images are processed locally and never sent to us. Gumroad's handling of your purchase is covered by Gumroad's privacy policy.

6. Children

Diamond Painting Pattern Maker does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For important changes we will also let you know in the app or on its Google Play listing before they take effect.

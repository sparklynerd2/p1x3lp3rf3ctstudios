Diamond Painting: Gem Coloring

Delete your account

Applies to the Android app Diamond Painting: Gem Coloring (com.diamondpixelations.app) · Last updated: November 9, 2026

You can ask us to delete your Diamond Painting: Gem Coloring account and all the data synced with it at any time, even if you no longer have the app installed.

Never signed in?

Then there's nothing on our servers to delete. As a guest, your progress lives only on your phone. Uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings › Apps › Diamond Painting: Gem Coloring › Storage › Clear data, to remove it permanently.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (user ID, email address and display name).
  • All painting progress and recently opened pictures saved to your account in Cloud Firestore.
  • The link between your account and Google Analytics for Firebase events (we remove the user ID association).

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. Data stored on your own device isn't affected: uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove it. Residual copies in encrypted backups are removed within 30 days.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you, except anything we're legally required to keep. Purchase records held by Google Play are managed by Google.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form below and send the email it creates, from the email address you signed in with. That's how we confirm the request is yours.
  2. We delete your account and data and email you to confirm, within 30 days and usually much sooner.

Request deletion

Send the request from this address so we can verify it's you.

This opens your email app with the request filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send.

Prefer to write it yourself? Email support@p1x3lp3rf3ctstudios.com with the subject Delete my Gem Coloring account.

Mega Game Break

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Mega Game Break handles, why, and the choices you have. Mega Game Break is made by P1x3l P3rf3ct Studios ("we", "us").

The short version

  • The app runs entirely on your device: no account, no analytics, no ads.
  • It has no internet access at all, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • Your puzzle progress is saved only on your phone.
  • We collect no personal information.

1. Information we collect

None. Mega Game Break works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting tools, and it does not send any information to us or to anyone else. All of its puzzles and pictures are built into the app, so it never needs to go online.

2. What stays on your device

So you can carry on where you left off, the app saves on your device which puzzles you have solved and your best time on each, any puzzle you are part-way through, and the last pack, level or tab you looked at. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes all of this.

3. Device backup

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app's saved progress in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account and its privacy policy; we cannot see it. You can turn backup off in Android Settings.

4. Links that open your browser

A few buttons open a web page in your browser: Photo source, shown when you finish a jigsaw made from a credited photograph, and the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service links on the home screen. Those pages are visited by your browser, not the app, and other websites are covered by their own privacy policies.

5. Device permissions

The app requests no permissions at all. It does not ask for internet access, and it has no access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

6. Purchases

The app is free. It has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads.

7. Children

Mega Game Break is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For important changes we will also let you know in the app or on its Google Play listing before they take effect.

Mega Game Break

Four kinds of puzzle in one calm app

Over 1,700 puzzles to unwind with: 229 picture jigsaws in ten themed packs, and 500 each of Sudoku, Mahjong and crosswords, all set against slowly moving scenes of misty mountains, a quiet reef and the northern lights.

  • Plays offline, with new puzzles downloaded when you are online
  • No account needed
  • Optional leaderboards
  • Free, with a one-time No ads option
Get it on Google Play

What's inside

Jigsaw puzzles229 pictures in ten packs, from dragons and fairies to forests and deep space, cut into 12 to 216 pieces.
Sudoku500 puzzles from Easy to Expert, each with exactly one solution, with notes, undo and hints.
Mahjong500 tile-matching puzzles across ten layouts, every deal guaranteed to be clearable.
Crosswords500 crosswords in the same ten themes as the jigsaws, at Easy, Hard and Expert.

Every puzzle saves your progress as you go, so you can stop at any time and pick up where you left off.

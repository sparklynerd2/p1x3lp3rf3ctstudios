Mega Puzzle Break

Delete your account or data

Applies to the Android app Mega Puzzle Break (com.megapuzzlebreak.app) · Last updated: November 21, 2026

You can ask us to delete your Mega Puzzle Break account, the progress synced with it and your leaderboard entries at any time, even if you no longer have the app installed. Or, if you'd like to keep your account, you can ask us to delete just your data.

Never signed in or joined a leaderboard?

Then there's nothing on our servers to delete. You can play Mega Puzzle Break without an account, and your progress lives only on your phone. Uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings › Apps › Mega Puzzle Break › Storage › Clear data, to remove it permanently.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account: the anonymous ID created when you joined the leaderboards and, if you signed in with Google, the Google email address, name and account ID linked to it.
  • The copy of your progress synced to your account (solved puzzles, best times, Traitor Word stats and nickname).
  • All your leaderboard entries (your nickname and best times).

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. Data stored on your own device isn't affected: uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove it.

Delete your data but keep your account

Choose Delete my data but keep my account in the form below. We delete your synced progress and all your leaderboard entries, but keep your account, so you can still sign in with Google and your nickname and leaderboard settings stay linked to it.

The app syncs whatever is on your phone back to your account when you're signed in. If you want a fresh start, clear the app's data on each phone you've signed in on (Android Settings › Apps › Mega Puzzle Break › Storage › Clear data) before or soon after you send the request; otherwise your progress comes back the next time you open the app.

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you, except anything we're legally required to keep. Your No ads purchase is recorded by Google Play under your Google account, not by us, so it isn't affected and still comes back if you reinstall. Information Google collects for ads is handled by Google; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form below and send the email it creates. If you signed in with Google, send it from that Google email address; that's how we confirm the request is yours.
  2. If you only joined the leaderboards without signing in, include your leaderboard nickname so we can find your entries.
  3. We delete your account and data (or just your data, if that's what you chose) and email you to confirm, within 30 days and usually much sooner.

Request deletion

If you signed in with Google, use that address and send the request from it.

This opens your email app with the request filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send.

Prefer to write it yourself? Email support@p1x3lp3rf3ctstudios.com with the subject Delete my Mega Puzzle Break account, and include your leaderboard nickname if you have one.

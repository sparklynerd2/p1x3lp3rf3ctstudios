Mega Puzzle Break

Six kinds of puzzle in one calm app

Over 2,000 puzzles to unwind with: hundreds of picture jigsaws, and 500 each of word searches, crosswords, Sudoku and Mahjong, plus endless games of The Traitor Word, set against calm zen gardens.

  • Plays offline, with new puzzles downloaded when you are online
  • Optional Google sign-in to sync your progress between devices
  • Online leaderboards
  • Free, with a one-time No ads option
Get it on Google Play

What's inside

Jigsaw puzzlesHundreds of pictures in themed packs, from dragons and fairies to zen gardens and deep space, cut into 12 to 216 pieces, with new ones added online.
Word searches500 word searches, each on its own topic, with 20 words to find.
Sudoku500 puzzles, each with exactly one solution, with notes, undo and hints.
Mahjong500 tile-matching puzzles across ten layouts, every deal guaranteed to be clearable.
Crosswords500 general knowledge crosswords.
The Traitor WordGuess the five-letter word in six tries, but one tile in every game lies. Expose it with your single accusation.

Every puzzle saves your progress as you go, so you can stop at any time and pick up where you left off. Sign in with Google in Records to keep your progress and best times on every phone.

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information The Traitor Word handles, why, and the choices you have. The Traitor Word is made by P1x3l P3rf3ct Studios ("we", "us").

The short version

  • The game runs entirely on your device: no account, no analytics, no ads.
  • Your stats are saved only on your phone.
  • We collect no personal information.

1. Information we collect

None. The Traitor Word works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting tools, and it does not send any information to us or to anyone else. The word lists are built into the app, so it doesn't even go online to play.

2. What stays on your device

Your game statistics (games played, wins, streaks and guess distribution) are saved on your device so they are there next time you play. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes them.

3. Sharing your results

If you tap Share, the app creates a text grid of coloured squares (no letters) and hands it to Android's share sheet or copies it to your clipboard. Nothing is shared unless you choose to, and it goes only where you send it.

4. Device permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files. Android lists an Internet permission because the game is built on a web view, but the app does not use it to send your data anywhere.

5. Purchases

If you buy the app or anything in it through Google Play, Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

6. Children

The Traitor Word does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For important changes we will also let you know in the app or on its Google Play listing before they take effect.
